About GeoX

Learn from someone who works in the field

GeoX Spatial Solution is built and taught by Md Yeamun Hasan Soumick, an M.Sc. graduate in Remote Sensing & GIS from Jahangirnagar University, currently working as a GIS Data Analyst on the HaRPI Project with the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT), and recognized as an Open Mapping Guru at the Open Mapping Hub Asia-Pacific.

That real-world, humanitarian mapping experience shapes every video — practical workflows, real tools, and skills that transfer directly to classwork, research, and jobs in the geospatial field.

  • GIS Data Analyst, HaRPI Project — Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team (HOT)
